ACADEMICS

Home • Academics

Basharat Higher Secondary School is an accredited Cambridge School and Cambridge Examination Centre, as well as a provider of FBISE and Aga Khan FSc and Matric options in senior years. We are committed to an academic programme that challenges students and prepares them for seamless transition between year levels throughout our three schools: Junior, Prep and Senior. All teaching is shaped by modern pedagogy and subject departments are led by well qualified and highly experienced Heads of Department supported by Academic Coordinators and Deputy Heads reporting to each Head of School. We drive learning through creative, activity based classes, especially in Junior and Prep, in which concepts underpin knowledge acquisition and application.

Read More →

Basharat Higher Secondary School has been a Cambridge school since 1988 and offers this curriculum in all three schools: Junior, Prep and Senior. We are also an official Cambridge Examination Centre. We also offer FBISE Matric and FSc in senior, thus enabling students to choose the most suitable curriculum for university and specialised courses.

Basharat H.S.S has its own SAT Centre and is affiliated with the American College Board.

Our Cambridge pedagogy is heavily influenced by international learner profiles and an explorative approach to teaching and learning. Classrooms are seen as dynamic places, although learning also takes place outside the classroom to expose students to practical and collaborative tasks. Teachers undertake regular professional development and there is a solid core of Cambridge Master Trainers in each of our schools. Academic departments are led by highly qualified and experienced men and women. All teachers possess a minimum Master's degree in their teaching discipline.

Each Head in Junior, Prep and Senior School is assisted by a Deputy, Year Level Coordinators and Heads of Department with support staff. Junior School has Heads of Lower and Upper sections, acknowledging a separation between early childhood development and older student expectations.

Boarders at Basharat Higher Secondary School receive evening academic tutorials.

Science Innovation encourages senior students to explore AI Robotics and Programming, Astronomy (Astrophysics), Environmental Science, and Principles of Biochemistry. Basharat Higher Secondary School is committed to promoting curiosity beyond the formal curriculum. The Basharat Science & Innovation Centre is a dedicated building for these purposes. Students may pursue these interests in spare lessons and after school. The Innovation Program is particularly useful for students wishing to assemble resumes for science and technology based courses at university level.

Pedagogical Innovation

The Harkness Discussion Method

Originating from the finest academies in the world, the Harkness Method replaces traditional lecture-based teaching with student-led collaborative discourse. Classes of 12 to 14 scholars sit around an oval mahogany table with the instructor acting as a moderator rather than an authoritarian lecturer.

Independent Intellectual Voice: Every student is encouraged to articulate, defend, and critically evaluate ideas in literature, history, and ethics.
Respectful Disagreement & Rhetoric: Cultivating active listening, rhetorical diplomacy, and collective problem-solving.
Pre-Collegiate Preparation: Directly emulating Oxford, Cambridge, and Ivy League seminar environments.
Senior scholars deliberating literary critique around the Harkness Table.
Student-moderated discussions developing critical metacognitive skills.
Standardized Testing

Official American College Board SAT Centre

Basharat Higher Secondary School is an official approved test centre for the American College Board (Code: #4108). We facilitate both internal pupils and external candidates sitting the Digital SAT (Scholastic Assessment Test).

Digital Testing Lab: High-speed secure testing suites adhering to strict College Board administrative protocols.
Dedicated SAT Math & Verbal Prep: In-house preparatory masterclasses for scholars aiming for 1500+ scores.
US University Placement Guidance: Direct advising on Common App, admissions essays, and merit scholarships.
Higher-Order Thinking

Meta Cognition - The BHSS Dialectic

Meta Cognition—learning how to think, evaluate thought processes, and adapt problem-solving strategies—is woven into the academic fabric of Basharat Higher Secondary School.

Through the BHSS Dialectic, students engage with epistemology, logical fallacies, hypothesis formulation, and ethical implications of scientific discoveries, empowering them to become original thinkers.

Honours & Laurels

Academic Distinctions & Student Recognition

Academic distinction is celebrated through the historic School Roll of Honour, Academic Blazers, Cambridge Outstanding Learner Awards, and BISE Board Position Trophies.

Top in Country Awards: Consistently producing nationwide distinctions in Cambridge IGCSE, O Levels & A Levels.
Board Position Holders: Gold & Silver medals in FBISE Pre-Medical and Pre-Engineering examinations.
The Principal's Academic Medal: Conferred annually upon the valedictorian at Speech Day.
Pre-University Milestone

A2 Level & Higher Secondary College Wing

The final year at Basharat Higher Secondary School represents the culmination of a scholar's secondary education. A2 Level and FSc Part-II students enjoy dedicated study suites, intensive evening tutorials, and university entrance test modules (MDCAT, ECAT, SAT).

Our graduates proceed directly into prestigious universities worldwide, including King Edward Medical, NUST, LUMS, Oxford, Cambridge, and Ivy League institutions.
