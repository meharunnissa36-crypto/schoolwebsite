ACADEMICS

Home • Academics

Basharat Higher Secondary School is an accredited educational institution affiliated with the Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (BISE) Gujranwala (BISE GRW). We are committed to an academic programme that instills conceptual mastery, analytical thinking, and ethical character (tarbiyah) throughout our four academic divisions: Primary School (Playgroup to Grade 5), Middle School (Grades 6 to 8), High School (Grades 9 & 10 / Matric), and Higher Secondary School for Girls (Grades 11 & 12 / Intermediate - FSc & ICS).

All instruction is led by well-qualified, experienced subject specialists and coordinators. We drive learning through concept-first classes in Primary and Middle, transitioning into rigorous board exam preparation and laboratory practicals in High School and Higher Secondary.

Institutional Board Centre

Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education (BISE) Gujranwala

Basharat Higher Secondary School Khewa is proudly affiliated with the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education (BISE) Gujranwala (BISE GRW). Since 1988, our institution has stood as an academic beacon in District Mandi Bahauddin, preparing thousands of successful scholars for Matriculation and Intermediate board qualifications.

BISE GRW Affiliation: Registered institutional examination center offering Secondary (SSC) and Higher Secondary (HSSC) curricula.
Comprehensive Syllabus Coverage: Punjab Curriculum and Textbook Board (PCTB) textbooks supplemented with teacher-curated conceptual worksheets and past-paper guides.
Pre-Board Mock Testing: Structured monthly revision tests and full-scale pre-board examinations matching official BISE GRW paper patterns and marking schemes.
Evening Supervised Study: Dedicated afternoon coaching and supervised evening study for board scholars ensuring individual attention.
Historic 100% Result in 9th Class Examination 2026 (BISE Gujranwala).
Board Position Holders Roll of Honour: Imama Zainab, Muhammad Zaka Ullah & Muskan Rani.
Grades 9 & 10 • SSC Science & CS

High School (Matriculation – BISE Gujranwala)

Our High School wing delivers focused, systematic preparation for 9th and 10th Class Secondary School Certificate (SSC) board examinations under BISE Gujranwala. We offer two premier science disciplines:

Science Group with Biology: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, English, Urdu, Islamiat, and Pakistan Studies for pre-medical aspirants.
Science Group with Computer Science: Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science, Mathematics, and general core subjects for tech and engineering aspirants.
Mandatory Laboratory Practicals: Comprehensive hands-on practical notebooks and apparatus drills matching BISE GRW practical exam standards.
100% Board Pass Track Record: Consistent regional records in 9th & 10th class exams with distinctions across Mandi Bahauddin district.
Students conducting physics and chemistry practicals in campus laboratories.

Grades 11 & 12 • HSSC Pre-Med, Pre-Eng & ICS

Higher Secondary School for Girls (Intermediate – BISE GRW)

Basharat Higher Secondary School for Girls provides an elite collegiate environment for 1st Year (11th) and 2nd Year (12th) female students. Affiliated with BISE Gujranwala, we offer specialized pre-university streams:

FSc Pre-Medical: Physics, Chemistry, Biology — in-depth conceptual lectures paired with early orientation for medical entrance tests (MDCAT).
FSc Pre-Engineering: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics — rigorous problem solving preparing scholars for leading engineering universities (ECAT).
ICS (Computer Science): Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics / Statistics — foundation for Software Engineering, Data Science, and Computer Science degrees.
Secure Environment & Van Transport: Safe, disciplined female campus wing with dedicated female coordinators and reliable van transport service.

Modern STEM & Technology

Computer & Python Coding Lab

At Basharat School System Khewa (Mandi Bahauddin), digital literacy, logical reasoning, and computer programming are integrated into the curriculum from Middle School. Our dedicated, air-cooled Computer & Python Coding Lab equips students with hands-on skills that bridge the gap between classroom theory and real-world technology careers.

Python Programming Fundamentals: Syntax, control structures, loops, functions, and algorithmic problem-solving taught from Grade 6 to Grade 10.
Digital Literacy & Touch Typing: Typing speed mastery, word processing, database concepts, and presentation design.
ICS & Board Computer Science: Comprehensive theoretical and practical preparation for BISE Gujranwala Matric & Intermediate Computer Science examinations.
Modern Computer & Python Coding Lab: Students mastering programming skills at Khewa campus.
Roll of Honour: Academic distinction combining STEM competence with top board marks.
Practical Experimentation

Physics, Chemistry & Biology Science Laboratories

Scientific education at Basharat Higher Secondary School is rooted in empirical discovery. Our purpose-built laboratories are equipped with modern apparatus, optical microscopes, chemical reagents, and circuit test-benches adhering to high safety standards.

Biology Laboratory: High-resolution optical microscopes, preserved biological specimens, histological slides, and human anatomical models.
Chemistry Laboratory: Fume hoods, precision electronic balances, titration apparatus, glassware, and safe chemical storage.
Physics Laboratory: Optical benches, vernier calipers, screw gauges, spectrometer prisms, and electrical circuit demonstration kits.
Hands-on chemical experiments and analytical titration drills.
