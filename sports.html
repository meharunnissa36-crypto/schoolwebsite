SPORTS

Home • Sports

Basharat Higher Secondary School offers a wide variety of sports with excellent coaching and playing facilities. Games and sports are played for enjoyment, but, although there is always a strong sense of competitiveness in both house and school fixtures, the aim of the Sports Department is to engage all boys in some form of physical activity.

Being part of a team not only helps in action, health, fitness and staying active, but also imparts confidence, discipline and teamwork. Sport is an indispensable part of a general boarding education.

The College has many specialist coaches, PT staff and support staff to manage a year-round school programme under the direction of the Games Master and Heads of School Sport.

Basharat College Cross Country Champion Sajjad Ali Khan running through the cheering avenue to become back-to-back champion of the prestigious Challenge Cup (2024 & 2025).

Facilities include:

  • Riding School (24 cottages, 400 jogging rings, show jumping and dressage arena, polo field)
  • 4 Cricket Pavilions (1st XI, Junior XI & Oval pavilions)
  • 5 Cricket Grounds (1st XI, ACO Oval, Butler's Oval, Swimming Pavilion & Principal's Oval)
  • Cricket Academy (8 synthetic nets and bowling machines)
  • Synthetic Hockey Field and Pavilion
  • 2 x 8-Lane Swimming Pools and Diving Complex (5-metre tower and springboards)
  • 4 Football Fields
  • 1 Rugby Field
  • 4 Tennis Courts Complex
  • 16 Table Tennis sets
  • 4 Basketball Courts with shooting practice facility
  • Hall for Indoor Training
  • Shooting Range
  • Grounds and gardens are kept immaculately by a committed and skilled team of groundsmen.
