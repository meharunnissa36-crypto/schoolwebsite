CLUBS & SOCIETIES

Home • Our Schools

There is a wide array of traditional and modern pursuits for boys to enjoy in all three schools. Clubs and Societies form a major part of university applications in Senior School, where there are more than twenty clubs and societies operating. Our extensive grounds also enable interested boys to participate in the Horticultural, Ornithological and Bee clubs.

HEAD BOY & STUDENT LEADERS

Directory of All 62 Clubs & Societies

Every student at Basharat School System is encouraged to participate in co-curricular societies, building self-confidence, technological literacy, and moral character alongside academic excellence in BISE Gujranwala examinations.

Entrepreneurial Society

Startup incubations, venture pitches, and annual corporate mentorship summits.

Debating Union (English & Urdu)

Parliamentary debate, British Parliamentary format, and national championships.

Model United Nations (BHSS MUN)

Diplomatic simulation, resolution drafting, and international delegation training.

AI & Robotics Guild

Arduino, Raspberry Pi, automated robotics, and machine learning projects.

Horticultural & Botanical Club

Organic greenhouse management, rare flora conservation on 45-acre campus.

Ornithological Society

Campus birdwatching, migration tracking, and avian conservation registry.

Apiculture & Bee Club

Campus apiary maintenance, ecological pollination, and pure honey extraction.

Astronomy & Space Club

High-powered optical telescope observation, astrophotography, and cosmology.

Dramatics & Theatre Guild

Annual classical Shakespearean play, Urdu theatre, and monologue festivals.

Fine Arts & Calligraphy Guild

Watercolour, oil canvas, Nastaliq calligraphy, and national art gallery exhibits.

Music & Orchestra Society

Eastern classical instruments, Western guitar ensemble, and annual concerts.

Community Service & Red Crescent

Blood donation drives, disaster relief camps, and vocational literacy teaching.

Prep Science & STEM Explorers

Hands-on physics experiments, rocketry, and digital electronics kits.

Prep Junior Debating Union

Introductory public speaking, declamations, and structured debate motions.

Prep Chess & Strategy Club

Tactical chess puzzles, opening theory, and inter-house blitz tournaments.

Junior Nature & Eco Club

Campus flora identification, recycling campaigns, and eco-conservation.

Prep Young Authors Guild

Short story writing, poetry anthology, and school newsletter publication.

Digital Photography Club

Composition rules, portrait capture, and campus life photojournalism.

Origami & Paper Sculpting

Spatial geometry, hand-eye coordination, and creative 3D paper crafts.

Spelling Bee & Phonics Club

Vocabulary building, etymology, and annual inter-house spelling bees.

Junior Lego & Mechanics

Introductory engineering mechanics, motorized Lego gears, and building.

Junior Clay & Pottery Arts

Tactile clay modeling, ceramic painting, and pottery wheel exploration.

Junior Gardening Sprouts

Planting flower beds, watering, seed germination, and nature awareness.

Junior Choral Singing

Vocal pitch, national anthems, melodic rhythm, and choir performances.
