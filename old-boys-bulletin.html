Volume 32 • Number 1

Official Publication of the Old Boys' Association

This edition of the Old Boys' Bulletin documents the major achievements of our distinguished alumni, annual reunions, school heritage reports, and special feature interviews.

Hard copies are distributed to alumni across Pakistan, the United Kingdom, the United States, and the UAE. Digital reprints are maintained in the Basharat Higher Secondary School Archives.
