Est. 1988 • Khewa, Mandi Bahauddin

A Heritage of Academic Distinction & Moral Tarbiyah

For nearly four decades, Basharat Higher Secondary School has stood as a beacon of scholarly achievement and character formation in District Mandi Bahauddin. Affiliated with the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education (BISE) Gujranwala (BISE GRW), our institution provides a seamless educational pathway across four dedicated wings: Primary School (Playgroup–5), Middle School (Grades 6–8), High School (Grades 9–10 Matric Science & CS), and Higher Secondary School for Girls (FSc Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering & ICS).

BISE GRW Affiliated

Accredited board curriculum with top marks and 100% board pass track record.

Expert Faculty

Experienced subject masters committed to analytical concept building.

Girls College Wing

Dedicated, secure Higher Secondary campus for FSc & ICS intermediate.

Moral Tarbiyah

Islamic character building, discipline, civic manners, and lifelong values.

Collegiate Wing Distinction

Empowering Female Scholars in FSc & ICS

Basharat Higher Secondary School for Girls provides a dedicated, culturally dignified, and academically rigorous environment in Khewa, Mandi Bahauddin. Led by experienced board faculty, our scholars excel in FSc Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, and ICS with 100% board pass rates and university admissions.
