Est. 1988 • Khewa, Mandi Bahauddin

A Heritage of Academic Distinction & Moral Tarbiyah

Basharat Higher Secondary School has been a cornerstone of quality education in District Mandi Bahauddin for nearly four decades. Affiliated with the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education (BISE) Gujranwala (BISE GRW), our institution provides an unbroken educational pathway structured across four dedicated divisions: Primary School (Playgroup–5), Middle School (Grades 6–8), High School (Grades 9–10 Matric Science & CS), and Higher Secondary School for Girls (FSc Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering & ICS).
