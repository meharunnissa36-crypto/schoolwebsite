DAY BOARDING & CAMPUS SERVICES

Home • Day Boarding & Student Services

Boarding at Basharat Higher Secondary School is a rich, transformative tradition that forms the bedrock of character education, leadership, and lifelong brotherhood. Living on campus immerses students in an environment of scholarly rigour, athletic discipline, and cultural distinction under the guidance of resident Housemasters, tutors, and pastoral caregivers.

Our boarding community is organised into distinguished, historic and purpose-built houses catering to junior, prep, and senior boarding scholars. Each house fosters its own proud spirit, traditions, and inter-house competitions, while upholding shared values of integrity, empathy, and service.

Campus Life, Day Boarding & Student Services

Comprehensive educational care: supervised evening study coaching, outstation hostel accommodation, safe van transport fleet, and nutritious dining.

A Boarder's Daily Schedule

A structured routine balancing rigorous academics, physical fitness, cultural refinement, and restful leisure.

Time Activity Description & Supervision
06:00 AM Rising Bell & Conditioning Morning run, physical fitness drills, and exercises with PT instructors.
07:15 AM Central Mess Breakfast Hot nutritious breakfast with fresh farm dairy, cereals and fruit.
08:00 AM Morning Assembly & Classes Full academic curriculum in Junior, Prep, and Senior Wings.
01:30 PM Formal Lunch & Rest Hour House dining followed by mandatory quiet period in boarding houses.
03:30 PM Afternoon Sports & Clubs Cricket, athletics, badminton, children's playground, coding lab, and debating.
05:30 PM Evening Refreshments & Leisure Tea and light refreshments in common rooms, indoor games, table tennis, and campus walks.
06:30 PM First Prep (Supervised Study) Dedicated academic study with resident subject specialists.
08:00 PM Formal House Dinner Three-course dinner in formal attire with housemasters.
08:45 PM Second Prep & Quiet Reading Independent study, university applications, or library reading.
09:45 PM House Call-Over & Prayers Roll-call, notices, personal reflections, and prayer assembly.
10:15 PM Lights Out Rest for Junior & Prep. Senior scholars extend to 11:00 PM.

Resident Housemasters

Dedicated senior faculty living on-site in each house, providing continuous pastoral care, character mentorship, and parental communication.

24/7 Medical Care & Hospital

A fully equipped campus health centre with resident qualified medical officers, round-the-clock nursing care, and dedicated emergency ambulance support.

Nutritious Messing

Wholesome meals formulated by certified nutritionists, featuring organic farm produce, fresh bakery items, and special diet consideration.

Gated 24/7 Security

Gated and monitored campus perimeter with trained security personnel, round-the-clock CCTV surveillance, and vetted visitor protocols.
