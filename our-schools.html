OUR SCHOOLS

Home • Our Schools

Basharat School System Khewa (Mandi Bahauddin) is the premier educational institution in the district, dedicated to academic distinction, modern scientific inquiry, and holistic character building. Affiliated with the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education (BISE) Gujranwala, our school provides an unbroken continuum of quality education from Early Childhood (Playgroup) through Matriculation (Science & Computer Science) and Higher Secondary (FSc Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering & ICS).

Our modern multi-story campus, located on Mandi Malakwal Road near Khewa Pull, is equipped with purpose-built facilities designed to nurture 21st-century minds: state-of-the-art Physics, Chemistry, and Biology Science Laboratories, an air-cooled Computer & Python Coding Lab, modern multimedia classrooms, and vibrant outdoor play areas featuring children's slides, swings, and roundabout. Our outstanding faculty combines rigorous pedagogical coaching with moral tarbiyah, evidenced by our historic 100% Board Result in the 9th Class Examination 2026.

Beyond academics, Basharat School System provides comprehensive student transport coverage connecting Khewa, Mandi Bahauddin city, and surrounding rural communities, alongside structured Day Boarding & Supervised Study Coaching that guarantees individual academic attention for every scholar.

Student Governance

Head Boy, Head Girl & Prefectorial Board

Leadership is a defining hallmark of Basharat Higher Secondary School. The Prefectorial Board represents the pinnacle of student governance, charged with upholding tradition, maintaining discipline, and fostering fraternity among all year groups.

The Prefectorial Council: Selected annually based on academic excellence, athletic contributions, and impeccable moral character.
House Captains & Monitors: Leading our historic 4-House system in inter-house sports, declamation cups, and social welfare drives.
Student Council Assemblies: Bi-weekly consultations with the Principal and Dean of Students to represent student voice.
Annual Investiture Ceremony & Badge Presentation at Jinnah Hall.
Student Council coordinating the All-Pakistan Youth Summit.
Holistic Wellbeing

Pastoral Care, Boarding & Career Counseling

At Basharat H.S.S, every child is known, valued, and nurtured. Our pastoral structure ensures that resident and day pupils receive comprehensive guidance across their emotional, intellectual, and physical growth.

Resident Housemasters & Tutors: Living in dedicated boarding quarters to provide 24/7 care, academic assistance, and warmth.
University Placement Office: Dedicated college counselors guiding scholars towards top medical, engineering, and Ivy/Russell Group universities.
Campus Health Clinic: Round-the-clock medical staff and resident physician overseeing pupil wellness.
Ages 14 – 18

Senior School (Matric, FSc & Cambridge)

The Senior School at Basharat Higher Secondary School provides an intellectually rigorous environment preparing students for national board distinctions and international qualifications.

BISE Matriculation & Intermediate: FSc Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, ICS Computer Science, and Humanities with dedicated entry test coaching.
Cambridge Assessment Stream: Cambridge O Levels and A Levels with comprehensive laboratory practicals.
Ages 11 – 13

Preparatory School (Grades 6 to 8)

Prep School acts as a bridge between child discovery and mature scholarship. Scholars develop self-discipline, inquiry-based scientific exploration, and compete in major inter-school sports.

Ages 5 – 10

Junior School (Grades 1 to 5)

Junior School is set in its own safe, green quadrangle with sensory play gardens, interactive multimedia classrooms, and dedicated child psychologists ensuring joyful learning.

Athletic Excellence

Sports Grounds, Cricket Academy & Children's Playground

Sport is an integral pillar of character building at BHSS. Featuring dedicated cricket practice nets, a joyful children's adventure playground (slides, swings & carousel), athletic sprint tracks, and football grounds.
