OUR SCHOOLS

Home • Our Schools

Basharat School System Khewa (Mandi Bahauddin) is the premier educational institution in the district, dedicated to academic distinction, modern scientific inquiry, and holistic character building. Affiliated with the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education (BISE) Gujranwala (BISE GRW), our school provides an unbroken continuum of quality education from Early Childhood (Playgroup) through Matriculation (Science & Computer Science) and Higher Secondary (FSc Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering & ICS).

Our modern multi-story campus, located on Mandi Malakwal Road near Khewa Pull, is equipped with purpose-built facilities designed to nurture 21st-century minds: state-of-the-art Physics, Chemistry, and Biology Science Laboratories, an air-cooled Computer & Python Coding Lab, modern multimedia classrooms, and vibrant outdoor play areas featuring children's slides, swings, and roundabout. Our outstanding faculty combines rigorous pedagogical coaching with moral tarbiyah, evidenced by our historic 100% Board Result in the 9th Class Examination 2026.

Beyond academics, Basharat School System provides comprehensive student transport coverage connecting Khewa, Mandi Bahauddin city, and surrounding rural communities, alongside structured Day Boarding & Supervised Study Coaching that guarantees individual academic attention for every scholar.

Playgroup to Grade 5 • Foundational Years

Primary School (Playgroup – Grade 5)

The Primary School at Basharat School System Khewa provides a joyful, caring, and stimulating environment for young learners during their crucial formative years. Covering Playgroup, Nursery, Prep, and Grades 1 through 5, our early childhood pedagogy is built around activity-based learning, phonics literacy, and curiosity-driven discovery.

Activity-Based Cognitive Learning: Modern sensory play, flashcard storytelling, and manipulative kits that make numbers, shapes, and vocabulary engaging.
Phonics & Dual-Language Mastery: Strong foundation in spoken English fluency, Urdu reading, and neat cursive writing habits.
Moral Character & Akhlaq Tarbiyah: Cultivating Islamic etiquettes, empathy, truthfulness, and daily morning Nazra and Duas.
Joyful Adventure Playground: Dedicated children's play area with safe outdoor slides, swings, roundabout, and grassy courtyards for healthy recreation.
Bright, interactive primary classrooms designed for activity-based exploration.

Grades 6 to 8 • Concept Building & IT Skills

Middle School (Grades 6 to 8)

Middle School acts as the critical bridge connecting foundational primary schooling with high-stakes board examinations. For scholars in Grades 6, 7, and 8, our faculty emphasizes conceptual clarity, scientific reasoning, mathematical problem solving, and technological competence.

Integrated Science Disciplines: Early experimental demonstrations in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology to foster genuine scientific curiosity.
Computer & Python Coding Lab: Practical training in touch typing, digital literacy, and basic Python programming in our dedicated air-cooled lab.
Speech, Qirat & Essay Writing: Regular inter-class competitions fostering public speaking confidence, eloquent expression, and Islamic values.
Rigorous Assessment System: Structured weekly tests, monthly reviews, and parent consultation sessions to track individual academic growth.
Hands-on technology education in our modern Computer & Python Coding Lab.
Collaborative concept building and science practical demonstrations.
Grades 9 & 10 • Matriculation (BISE Gujranwala)

High School (Grades 9 & 10 / Matric – BISE GRW)

The High School at Basharat Higher Secondary School provides intensive, result-driven preparation for the Secondary School Certificate (SSC / Matriculation) examinations affiliated with the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education (BISE) Gujranwala (BISE GRW).

Science Group (Biology): Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, English, Urdu, Islamiat, and Pakistan Studies.
Science Group (Computer Science): Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science, Mathematics, and general subjects.
Fully-Equipped Laboratories: Dedicated science practical facilities for Physics, Chemistry, and Biology experiments conducted under expert instructors.
Historic 100% Board Pass Record: Proven distinction in BISE Gujranwala 9th and 10th Class Examinations with top regional scorers (Imama Zainab 516/555, Muhammad Zaka Ullah 513/555, Muskan Rani 499/555).

Grades 11 & 12 • FSc Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering & ICS

Higher Secondary School for Girls (BISE GRW)

Basharat Higher Secondary School for Girls, established in 1988 in Khewa, Mandi Bahauddin, is the premier collegiate institution of the region. Affiliated with the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education (BISE) Gujranwala (BISE GRW), our Higher Secondary Wing provides an empowering, safe, and academically rigorous environment tailored specifically for young women aspiring to enter top medical, engineering, software, and higher education universities.

FSc Pre-Medical: Physics, Chemistry, Biology — rigorous board curriculum paired with conceptual preparation for medical college entry tests (MDCAT).
FSc Pre-Engineering: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics — deep analytical problem-solving preparing female engineers for leading engineering universities.
ICS (Computer Science): Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics / Statistics — modern programming and software foundation for BS Computer Science and Software Engineering careers.
Secure Campus & Safe Transport: Dedicated girls wing with female administration support, strictly supervised discipline, and door-to-door safe school van transport across Mandi Bahauddin and Khewa.
Female Scholars: FSc Pre-Medical & ICS students in academic seminar library.
Dedicated collegiate environment empowering female leaders for pre-university success.
Athletic Development & Recreation

Sports Grounds, Cricket Academy & Children's Playground

Physical fitness, discipline, and healthy sportsmanship are fundamental pillars at Basharat Higher Secondary School. Our campus boasts dedicated sports facilities serving all year groups:

Cricket Pitch & Practice Nets: Dedicated cricket coaching nets for bowling and batting practice under physical training instructors.
Multi-Purpose Football & Athletics Ground: Grassy pitch for football matches, 100m/200m sprint tracks, and physical training drills.
Badminton, Volleyball & Table Tennis: Court games promoting teamwork, hand-eye coordination, and active reflexes.
Children's Adventure Playground: Safe outdoor recreational park featuring slides, swings, and roundabout for Primary School students.
