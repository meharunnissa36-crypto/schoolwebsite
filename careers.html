Join Our Team at Basharat Higher Secondary School

Basharat Higher Secondary School seeks exceptional educators and administrative professionals who are deeply committed to the education, well-being, and holistic development of boys. Whether teaching in the classroom or providing essential operational support, every member of our team plays a vital role in shaping the leaders of tomorrow.

Academic Faculty

We look for inspirational teachers who understand how to motivate boys and enrich their learning both within and beyond the classroom. Teachers at Basharat Higher Secondary School are expected to take an active interest in students' academic and personal growth, serving as exemplary mentors who build strong, positive relationships and shape character.

Administration

We seek forward-thinking administrative leaders and professionals who foster an organized, supportive, and efficient environment. Our administrators streamline operations, manage resources effectively, and uphold the college's rigorous standards, collaborating closely with staff, parents, and students to sustain a thriving community.

All applications must be submitted through the HR Office on hr@basharat.edu.pk. Contact by any other means will not be acknowledged. Please do not send applications or resumes to the Principal's Office.
